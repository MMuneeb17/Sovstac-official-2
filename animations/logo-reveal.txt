  THE IDEA
  The brackets you watch the whole time ARE the "[ ]" in the wordmark. They open
  on your line, seal it away, then the letters assemble around an EMPTY slot —
  and the frame flies into the hole it belongs in, completing S[]VSTAC.
  The frame never becomes the logo; it is revealed to have been it all along.

  Two facts make it work, both measured from the real artwork:
    · the bracket is 3 filled rects (spine 27u + two arms 22u), NEVER scaled —
      the spine and arm weights differ, so a scaleX would distort them
    · the letters are real Michroma, placed by measuring each glyph's ink box

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BEAT 5 — the empty slot  ⟨5.2s⟩  ease expo.out     timing: letterDur,
           (the hole)                                        letterStagger, holdEmpty
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

     the frame swings open again and the six Michroma letters assemble OUTWARD
     from the centre — S and V first, then STAC, the A last (its box overlaps
     the T by 49u, Michroma's own kern, so it settles IN, never crosses).

     for one held beat the wordmark sits there with a HOLE in it:

              S  ▯▯  V S T A C            S ▯ VSTAC  — nothing in the
                 ▯▯                        brackets' place. a gap where
                (empty)                    the "O" should be.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BEAT 6 — brackets fly in ⟨6.6s⟩  ease power4.inOut  timing: collapse, payoff
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

     the brackets sweep in from wide and SEAT into the slot. they are drawn
     BEHIND the letters, so they appear to travel behind the wordmark and
     surface only where nothing covers them — the hole they belong in.
     (the pair sits left of centre, so there is no width at which both clear
      the letters; painting them behind turns the collision into the effect.)

              S [] V S T A C                  S[]VSTAC — complete.
              ▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔                   accent brackets, pale letters,
              ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~  ⟨7.4s⟩          then a 1px hairline rule draws in.
