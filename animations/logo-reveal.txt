  THE IDEA
  The brackets you watch the whole time ARE the "[ ]" in the wordmark. They open
  on your line, seal it away, then sweep out to frame the WHOLE wordmark while
  the letters assemble inside them around an EMPTY slot — and fly back into the
  hole they belong in, completing S[]VSTAC.
  The frame never becomes the logo; it is revealed to have been it all along.

  Two facts make it work, both measured from the real artwork:
    · the bracket is 3 filled rects (spine 27u + two arms 22u), NEVER scaled —
      the spine and arm weights differ, so a scaleX would distort them
    · the letters are real Michroma, placed by measuring each glyph's ink box

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BEAT 5 — the full-frame reveal  ⟨4.1s⟩  ease power4.inOut
           (the empty slot)               timing: payoff, letterDur,
                                          letterStagger, holdEmpty
                                          geometry: revealMargin
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

     the frame sweeps out past BOTH ends of the wordmark — the whole of
     SOVSTAC will sit inside it. chamber + shift + scale + recentre are ONE
     tween, one ease: the brackets travel outward to their framing position
     as the stage settles down into logo size.

     (the pair lives left of centre, so the chamber's centre slides to the
      wordmark's centre while it opens — a symmetric opening could never
      clear the C. that slide is the `shift` state.)

     the six Michroma letters assemble INSIDE the wide-open frame, and a letter
     only EXISTS between the brackets — the aperture WIPES them into view as it
     opens across them. that's what makes the sweep a reveal, and it makes
     bracket-over-letter overlap impossible while the frame travels (the left
     bracket crosses the S's position mid-sweep). the gate comes off the instant
     the sweep completes. (A settles last — its box overlaps the T by 49u,
     Michroma's own kern, so it settles IN, never crosses.)

     for one held beat the wordmark sits there with a HOLE in it:

        [      S  ▯▯  V S T A C      ]        the whole text framed by the
                  (empty)                      brackets, revealMargin (70u) of
                                               clearance past each end — and
                                               nothing where the "O" should be.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BEAT 6 — brackets fly in ⟨6.1s⟩  ease power4.inOut  timing: collapse
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

     both brackets fly in from OUTSIDE the text: the left steps in past the S,
     the right sweeps in from beyond the C. they are drawn BEHIND the letters,
     so they travel behind the wordmark and surface only where nothing covers
     them — the hole they belong in. they seat at the same instant.

        [  ──▶    S ▯▯ VSTAC    ◀──  ]

              S [] V S T A C                  S[]VSTAC — complete.
              ▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔                   accent brackets, pale letters,
              ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~  ⟨6.8s⟩          then a 1px hairline rule draws in.
