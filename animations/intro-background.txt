  THE IDEA
  A field of particles drifts freely. Wherever the cursor goes, the particles
  near it are repelled OUTWARD into a glowing ring — a perimeter around a
  protected void. That ring is the brand's "[ ]" frame, drawn in motion:
  a boundary that forms around whatever you point at.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PARTICLE SHAPES               config: particleShape
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  capsule      ▬     the default rod, points at the cursor
  sphere       ●
  box          ▪
  tetrahedron  ▲
  bracket      [ ]   the brand mark itself — a full pair per particle
  bracket-single [   or   ]     lone brackets, randomly open/close

  With the bracket shapes, the big ring is made of hundreds of little "[ ]"s:
  the perimeter glyph, built out of perimeter glyphs.
